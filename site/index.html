Private by design, not by promise

  1. 1

    Locked on your Mac

    The library and every file are encrypted with a key in your Mac’s Keychain. A recovery key is the only other way in.

  2. 2

    Locked in the cloud too

    Voidcat Cloud is optional and end-to-end encrypted. Google Cloud in Belgium stores only locked bytes; your browser unlocks them with your recovery key.

  3. 3

    Masked before any AI

    Rules and an on-device privacy model replace names, IDs, addresses, emails and phone numbers with placeholders. Amounts stay, so the AI can still reason.
